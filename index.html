Attire Guide

Dress Code

Our celebration is dressed in warm, earthy hues. We kindly invite our guests to complement our rust and terracotta palette below.

Rust
Terracotta
Burnt Orange
Dusty Rose
Beige
Taupe
Sage Green
Cream

Principal Sponsors

Male

Beige, taupe, brown, or dark brown suit, with rust or terracotta tie or accents

Female

Elegant gown or cocktail dress in champagne/beige, terracotta/rust, or dusty rose/mauve

Other Guests

Male

Long sleeve, polo, or barong in beige, brown/terracotta, or sage green/olive

Female

Dress in earthy, warm, or soft tones — beige/champagne, rust/terracotta, dusty rose/mauve, or sage green/olive

Tips

  • Avoid overpowering neon or very bright colors.
  • Fabrics: linen, cotton, chiffon, satin, tulle.
  • Add subtle rust/terracotta accents (ties, pocket square, bouquet, accessories).

Formal attire is encouraged. We kindly ask that our guests refrain from wearing white, ivory, or champagne gowns, as these are reserved for the bride.
