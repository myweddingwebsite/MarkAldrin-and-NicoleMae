Attire Guide

Dress Code

Our celebration is dressed in warm, earthy hues. We kindly invite our guests to complement our rust and terracotta palette below.

Principal Sponsors

Male

Black suit

With or without rust / Terracota tie or accents

Female

Dusty Rose / Mauve / Rosewood / Soft blush

Elegant gown or cocktail dresses

Other Guests

Male

Beige, Brown / Terracotta, Sage Green / Olive

Long sleeve / polo / barong in neutral or earth tones

Female

Beige / Champagne, Terracotta, Sage Green / Olive

Dresses in earthy, warm, or soft tones — avoid bright colors

Tips

  • Avoid overpowering neon or very bright colors.
  • Fabrics: linen, cotton, chiffon, satin, tulle.
  • Add subtle rust/terracotta accents (ties, pocket square, bouquet, accessories).

Formal attire is encouraged. We kindly ask that our guests refrain from wearing white gowns, as these is reserved for the bride.
